Alignment Dashboard Page Learning Resource

This guide teaches users how the Alignment Dashboard exposes gaps between what the organization signals and what its structures support.

Purpose

The Alignment Dashboard reviews alignment pairs, integrity gaps, unresolved contradictions, and evidence linked to alignment analysis.

Use it to:

  • inspect alignment overview charts
  • review alignment metric pairs
  • compare alignment-side and target-side values
  • inspect survey contributors
  • review unresolved contradiction components
  • update contradiction review status and notes
  • inspect evidence linked to alignment contradictions

Alignment review is where symbolic claims meet structural reality.

Bridge Metric Role

Alignment Dashboard is a Bridge surface. It helps users test whether Stage A diagnostic reality and symbolic interpretation are coherent before the system moves into Stage B decisions or governance claims.

The key bridge signal is SYMBOLIC_STRUCTURAL_INTEGRITY_GAP. A large gap is not just a visual warning. It can mean:

  • symbolic legitimacy is stronger than the supporting structures;
  • structures may be stronger than people believe;
  • evidence sources are contradicting each other;
  • governance should qualify recommendations, summaries, and board-pack claims.

Use user/metrics/Metrics explained.md and user/metrics/Comprehensive Metric Reference.md for the Stage A / Stage B / Bridge metric classification.

Alignment Pairs

An alignment pair connects an alignment metric to a target structural or symbolic metric.

The gap shows how far the stated or perceived alignment differs from the supporting condition.

Large gaps should prompt questions such as:

  • Is the organization claiming progress that structures do not support?
  • Are employees reporting a different reality than leadership describes?
  • Is the evidence strong enough to explain the gap?

Contradiction Review

The unresolved contradictions section lets users update review status and notes for integrity-gap components.

Use review notes to explain:

  • what is contradicted
  • what evidence supports the contradiction
  • what evidence is missing
  • what follow-up is needed

Downstream Impact

Alignment Dashboard feeds:

  • Score Dashboard interpretation
  • Score Component Details
  • Evidence Notes
  • Recommendations
  • Governance Summary
  • board-pack qualification

Common Mistakes

Avoid treating alignment gaps as cosmetic.

Avoid resolving contradictions without evidence.

Avoid assuming survey contributors explain a gap by themselves.

Avoid hiding contradiction inside narrative text only.

Training Prompt

Ask the trainee to open one high-gap alignment pair, identify linked evidence, and write a contradiction review note explaining what must be re-tested.

Completion standard: the user can explain how alignment gaps qualify governance claims.
