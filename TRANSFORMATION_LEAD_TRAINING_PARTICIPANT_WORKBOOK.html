Transformation Lead Training Participant Workbook

WorkPlace Conditions Governance

How to use this workbook

This workbook is for Transformation Leads being trained to coordinate the full WorkPlace Conditions Governance operating loop.

It is not a replacement for the facilitator guide or the doctrine module. It is a practical workbook for use during:

  1. the half-day foundation session;
  2. the half-day applied workflow session;
  3. supervised use through at least one complete assessment cycle.

Use this workbook to write down your answers, classify what you see, and record what remains uncertain.

How this workbook fits the final training sequence

This workbook is for learner practice. It should be used after the management-purpose architecture has been introduced.

Use it alongside:

Important role boundary

This is not ordinary staff training.

Transformation Leads coordinate a deeper workflow than ordinary staff are expected to test.

Ordinary staff should not be asked to discover hidden prerequisite states or validate advanced governance logic by exploration.

If a required fixture or data condition is missing, record:

Not exercised — fixture unavailable.

Missing fixture data is not a defect.

Part 1 — Participant Orientation

What is the Transformation Lead role?

A Transformation Lead helps coordinate the path from diagnosis to accountable governance review.

The role is to help the organization move through this loop:

diagnosis
→ recommendation
→ continuity and carry-forward
→ structural decision
→ pilot and KPI linkage
→ scenario pressure and mitigation state
→ governance action ownership and closure
→ learning
→ review-pack preparation

Part 4 — Performance Ambiguity Worksheet

Key distinction

ModeMeaning
Presentational performanceAppropriate statements, gestures, reports, metrics, rituals, or compliance signals that appear aligned with values.
Value-realising performanceActual achievement of intended outcomes in relation to mission, dignity, trust, cooperation, collaboration, and real value creation.

Core question:

Are we observing a persuasive performance of alignment, or evidence that the intended value is actually being realised?

Classification activity

Classify each example.

ExamplePresentational performanceValue-realising performanceWhy?
A team completes every governance form but affected staff still distrust the process.
A manager uses correct inclusion language but dissenters are excluded from decision-making.
A pilot produces evidence that workload pressure has actually reduced.
A review pack looks complete but contradiction evidence is buried.
A conflict is openly contested, resolved legitimately, and follow-through occurs.
A dashboard metric improves because people stop reporting concerns.
A governance action is closed after evidence shows the underlying condition improved.
