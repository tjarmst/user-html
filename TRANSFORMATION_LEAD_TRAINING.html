Transformation Lead Training

WorkPlace Conditions Governance

Purpose

This resource trains Transformation Leads to coordinate the full WorkPlace Conditions Governance operating loop.

Transformation Leads require the deepest practical training because they coordinate the workflow across diagnosis, recommendations, structural decisions, pilots, KPIs, scenario interpretation, governance actions, closure, and review-pack preparation.

This resource supplements the existing training set. It does not replace the Training Manual, Concept Primer, Review-Page Operating Guides, or Role-Based Smoke-Test Scripts.

Recommended duration

ComponentDuration
Foundation session1 half-day
Applied workflow session1 half-day
Supervised practiceAt least one complete assessment cycle

Audience

This resource is for Transformation Leads who will:

  • coordinate an assessment cycle;
  • help move diagnostic findings into recommendations;
  • support recommendation workflow and continuity;
  • coordinate conversion into structural decisions;
  • connect pilots, KPIs, evidence, and lineage;
  • interpret scenario pressure and mitigation state;
  • manage governance action ownership and closure;
  • prepare governance review packs.

This is not ordinary staff training.

Critical rule:

> Ordinary staff should not be asked to discover hidden prerequisite states or validate advanced governance logic by exploration.

Session 1 — Half-Day Foundation

Learning objective

By the end of the foundation session, the Transformation Lead should be able to explain:

  1. what WorkPlace Conditions Governance is for;
  2. the full operating loop;
  3. key terminology;
  4. how evidence, contradiction, weak support, and supportable states differ;
  5. why role boundaries and fixture discipline matter.

Foundation agenda

SegmentTopicSuggested time
1Purpose and governance model30 min
2Full operating loop45 min
3Core terminology60 min
4Evidence, uncertainty, contradiction, and weak support45 min
5Role boundaries and testing discipline30 min
6Review and questions30 min

1. Purpose and governance model

WorkPlace Conditions Governance is not just a dashboard.

It is a governance workflow that helps an organization move from:

diagnosis
→ recommendation
→ structural decision
→ pilot / implementation
→ KPI and evidence monitoring
→ scenario pressure review
→ governance action ownership
→ learning and closure
→ review-pack preparation

The Transformation Lead coordinates this movement.

Metrics as Stage A, Stage B, and Bridge signals

Transformation Leads must be able to explain the metric system in plain management language:

  • Stage A metrics help the organization understand reality.
  • Stage B metrics and states test whether action is working.
  • Bridge metrics connect diagnosis, action, evidence, continuity, scenario pressure, and governance learning.

Teaching example:

SignalRoleInterpretation
structural_health_indexStage AWhat do workplace structures currently support or undermine?
KPI progress / pilot outcomeStage BIs the intervention producing measurable movement?
symbolic_structural_integrity_gap or lineage drop-offBridgeAre claims, structures, action, and evidence coherent?

The Transformation Lead should use this framing before asking users to interpret Score Dashboard, KPI Dashboard, Lineage View, or Governance Summary outputs.

2. Full operating loop

StageTransformation Lead question
DiagnoseWhat condition, risk, or structural concern has been identified?
RecommendWhat recommendation responds to it?
Check continuityIs this new, or does it continue prior work?
DecideHas the recommendation become a structural decision?
ExecuteIs there a pilot or implementation pathway?
Link KPIs/evidenceWhat shows whether it is working?
Review scenario pressureDoes future/contextual pressure change the interpretation?
Govern actionsWho owns follow-up, and what is its status?
Close learningWhat has been learned, and what remains unresolved?
Prepare review packWhat should governance bodies see and decide?

3. Core terminology

TermMeaningTransformation Lead question
RecommendationProposed governance response to a diagnosed concern.Is it clear enough to act on, link, review, and carry forward?
ContinuityRelationship between recommendations across cycles.Is this genuinely new or continuing prior work?
Carry-forwardCurrent recommendation continues a prior-cycle issue.Is there a valid predecessor?
Structural decisionA decision to preserve, reform, or replace a condition or pathway.Has the recommendation become a concrete decision?
PilotBounded trial or implementation pathway.What is being tried, owned, measured, and reviewed?
KPIMeasure used to assess outcome, threshold, or progress.Does this KPI help judge effect?
EvidenceSupport or qualification for interpretation.Is the evidence strong enough?
Scenario pressureFuture/contextual pressure affecting interpretation.Does this make current action inadequate, urgent, contradicted, or weak?
Governance actionOwned follow-up action.Who owns it, what happens next, and when can it close?

4. Evidence and scenario states

StateMeaning
Insufficient evidenceNot enough linked support to justify the conclusion.
Weak supportSome support exists, but it is partial, thin, or under pressure.
Blocking pressureScenario or governance pressure blocks easy closure.
SupportableCurrent evidence supports a bounded conclusion.
Attention promptGovernance attention is required.

Important:

A missing fixture is not a defect.
A weak state is not failure.
A supportable state is not certainty.

Normal LLM Support

Transformation Leads may use normal LLM support services for:

  • explaining visible page text;
  • summarising exported rows;
  • drafting review-pack notes;
  • converting observations into plain language;
  • preparing meeting summaries.

Safe prompt:

I am using WorkPlace Conditions Governance. Here is the visible page text/export. Please help me summarise it without assuming hidden data.

Do not ask an LLM to infer unseen application state.

Do not treat an LLM as connected to the live platform unless it actually is.
