Lineage View Page Learning Resource

This guide teaches users how the Lineage View traces recommendation-to-decision-to-pilot-to-KPI execution and qualifies that trace with evidence, learning, scenarios, and governance response.

Purpose

Lineage View shows whether governance work has a real execution chain.

Use it to:

  • inspect stage drop-off
  • review lineage diagnostics
  • inspect learning-state overlay
  • inspect evidence, contradiction, and incompleteness overlay
  • review weak-execution paths
  • relink existing evidence to lineage rows
  • inspect KPI threshold impact by domain
  • inspect learning-state timelines
  • review scenario-to-execution overlay
  • review governance-response overlay

Lineage is not just traceability. It helps determine whether a claim has execution support.

Bridge Role

Lineage View is a Bridge page. It asks whether Stage A understanding has become accountable Stage B action.

Lineage completeness or drop-off is a governance signal:

  • recommendation without decision means diagnosis may not have become choice;
  • decision without pilot means choice may not have become tested action;
  • pilot without KPI means action may not have measurable outcome evidence;
  • KPI without evidence or learning closure means measurement may not yet support governance learning.

This is why lineage belongs in the same metric-value story as integrity gap, execution feedback, scenario pressure, and overall flourishing interpretation.

Stage Drop-Off

Stage drop-off shows where work stops:

  • recommendation without decision
  • decision without pilot
  • pilot without KPI
  • KPI without evidence or learning closure

Drop-off does not always mean failure, but it requires interpretation.

Semantic And Review Context

The evidence overlay distinguishes:

  • stronger findings
  • advisory signals
  • contradictions
  • incompleteness
  • weak execution

After the semantic/context passes, lineage-related rows may expose review context, semantic lines, and routing/handling cues. Use those cues to avoid treating missing evidence as support.

Evidence Relinking

Use evidence relinking when an existing evidence note belongs with a lineage path.

Optional KPI linkage can connect the same evidence to a more specific outcome indicator.

Scenario And Governance Overlays

Scenario overlay shows whether decisions or pilots are linked to scenario pressure and whether mitigation claims are supported by execution evidence.

Governance-response overlay shows whether governance actions are linked to weak or risky paths.

Downstream Impact

Lineage View feeds:

  • Governance Execution Review
  • Scenario Effectiveness Review
  • Governance Summary
  • Comparative Cycle Review continuity interpretation
  • board-pack output

Common Mistakes

Avoid treating a visible chain as proof of effectiveness.

Avoid treating scenario linkage as mitigation evidence by itself.

Avoid ignoring weak-execution or incompleteness overlays.

Avoid relinking evidence without checking whether it supports, qualifies, or contradicts the path.

Training Prompt

Ask the trainee to trace one recommendation through decision, pilot, and KPI, then identify one evidence qualification and one possible drop-off risk.

Completion standard: the user can explain both the chain and the strength of evidence behind it.
